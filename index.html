Features

Fast while you puzzle. Detailed when you're done.

  • Puzzle catalog

    Title, manufacturer, piece count, type. Scan a barcode to prefill from our catalog or a UPC lookup.

  • Stopwatch timer

    Big buttons. Screen stays on. Pause, resume, finish. Works in landscape when you're mid-table.

  • Milestone markers

    Tap to drop a marker mid-solve — no typing. Label them after: border done, halfway, last hundred pieces.

  • Flip time

    Record when the flipping's done. Assembly time tracked separately from total solve time.

  • Stats & records

    Best times, averages, trends. See how this solve compares to every previous attempt on the same puzzle.

  • Ratings

    Score difficulty and fun 1–10 after each solve. Figure out what you actually enjoy building.

  • Photos & video

    Box shots, finished puzzle pics. Optional timelapse or solve recording from the camera.

  • Quick timer

    Start timing before you've picked a puzzle. Assign the solve to your collection later.

  • Date-only mode

    Not racing? Track start and finish dates without a stopwatch. Good for the slow-and-steady crowd.

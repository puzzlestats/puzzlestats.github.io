What it does

Fast while you're at the table. Detailed when you're done.

Catalog

Log title, brand, piece count, type. Scan barcodes to autofill from our built-in database.

Stopwatch

Big controls, screen stays on. Pause, resume, finish. Landscape mode at the puzzle table.

Markers

One tap mid-solve. Label later — border done, halfway, last hundred pieces.

Flip time

Record when flipping's done. Assembly time tracked as its own stat.

Stats

Best, average, trends. Compare each solve to your history on the same puzzle.

Ratings

Score difficulty and fun 1–10. Learn what you actually enjoy building.

Photos & video

Box shots and finished puzzle pics. Optional timelapse recording.

Quick timer

Start timing before you've picked a puzzle. Assign it to your collection later.

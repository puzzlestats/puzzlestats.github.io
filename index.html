What it does

  • Catalog

    Log puzzles with title, brand, piece count. Scan barcodes to autofill.

  • Timer

    Big stopwatch. Screen stays awake. Pause, resume, finish. Landscape works.

  • Markers

    Tap mid-solve to drop a marker. Name them after — border, halfway, last 100.

  • Flip time

    Hit a button when flipping's done. Assembly time tracked separately.

  • Stats

    Best, average, trends. Compare this solve to every past attempt on the same puzzle.

  • Ratings

    Score difficulty and fun 1–10. Figure out what you actually like building.

  • Photos & video

    Box shots, finished pics. Optional timelapse recording.

  • Quick timer

    Start before you've picked a puzzle. Sort it out later.

  • Date-only mode

    No stopwatch — just log start and finish dates.

Built for speed puzzlers

Every feature is designed around the way you actually puzzle — fast during solves, detailed afterward.

📚

Puzzle Catalog

Build your collection with title, manufacturer, piece count, and type. Scan barcodes to prefill from our bundled catalog or UPC lookup.

⏱️

Precision Timer

Large, distraction-free controls for start, pause, resume, and finish. Screen stays awake during active solves. Landscape mode supported.

📍

Milestone Markers

Tap to add markers mid-solve — no typing required. Label them afterward: border complete, halfway point, last 100 pieces, and more.

🔄

Flip Time Tracking

Record when you finish flipping pieces. Track assembly time separately from total solve time as a first-class statistic.

📊

Statistics & Records

Best times, averages, improvement trends, and personal records by piece count. Compare each solve to your history on the same puzzle.

⭐

Ratings

Rate difficulty and fun on a 1–10 scale after each solve. Track what you love and what challenges you most.

📷

Photos & Video

Capture box photos and completed puzzle shots. Optionally record solve videos or timelapses with the device camera.

⚡

Quick Timer

Start timing instantly without picking a puzzle. Assign the solve to your collection later from the home screen.

📅

Date-Only Tracking

Not racing the clock? Track start and end dates without a stopwatch — perfect for casual puzzlers who still want a history.

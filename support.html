FAQ

  • Do I need an account?

    No. Everything stays on your device. Optional iCloud Sync backs up to your own Apple devices.

  • Does it work offline?

    Yes — catalog, timer, markers, ratings, and stats all work without internet. You only need a connection for barcode lookups (when not in our catalog) and App Store purchases.

  • How do I sync across devices?

    Settings → iCloud Sync → enable. Same Apple ID on both devices. Restart the app after toggling. Photos and videos stay local for now.

  • What is date-only tracking?

    Turn off the stopwatch when starting a solve. It logs start and finish dates instead of elapsed time. Those solves don't count toward speed records.

  • How do markers work?

    Tap "Add Marker" mid-solve — no typing. After finishing, rename them, add notes, or apply templates like "Border Complete."

  • What is Quick Timer?

    Start timing without picking a puzzle first. The solve shows up on Home under "Solves Needing Attention" until you assign it.

  • How do I cancel a subscription?

    iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Puzzle Stats. Refunds are handled by Apple, not us.

  • How do I delete my data?

    Delete items in the app, or uninstall to remove everything local. Manage iCloud data through your Apple ID if sync was on.

  • Android?

    iOS only for now. Android is planned.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Puzzle Stats?

No. Puzzle Stats works entirely without an account. All your data is stored on your device. You can optionally enable iCloud Sync to back up and sync across your own Apple devices.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Core features — cataloging puzzles, timing solves, markers, ratings, and statistics — all work offline. Internet is only needed for barcode lookups when a UPC isn’t in the bundled catalog, and for iCloud Sync or App Store purchases.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Go to Settings → iCloud Sync and enable the toggle. You must be signed into the same Apple ID on both devices. Restart the app after changing this setting. Note that photos and videos are stored locally and are not synced via iCloud at this time.

What is date-only tracking?

When starting a solve, you can turn off speed puzzle timing. This records when you started and finished without a stopwatch — useful for casual puzzling. Date-only solves don’t count toward fastest or average time statistics.

How do markers work?

During a solve, tap “Add Marker” to record a milestone instantly — no typing needed. After finishing, you can rename markers, add notes, and apply templates like “Border Complete” or “Halfway Point.”

What is Quick Timer?

Quick Timer lets you start timing without selecting a puzzle first. When you finish, the solve appears under “Solves Needing Attention” on the home screen. You can then assign it to an existing puzzle or create a new one.

How do I scan a puzzle barcode?

When adding a puzzle, tap “Scan Barcode” at the top of the form. If the UPC matches our bundled catalog, the form prefills automatically. Otherwise, the app may look up the UPC online and suggest details you can edit before saving.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, then select Puzzle Stats to modify or cancel. We cannot process refunds directly — those are handled by Apple.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual puzzles, solves, photos, and videos within the app. To remove all data, delete the app from your device. If iCloud Sync was enabled, you may also want to manage CloudKit data through your Apple ID settings.

Is Android supported?

Puzzle Stats is currently available on iOS. Android is planned for a future release.
